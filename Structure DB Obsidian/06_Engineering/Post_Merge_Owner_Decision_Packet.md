Current closure proposal distinguishes a reviewed reported member from an accepted complete domain.
Exact-layer exclusion requires that layer's accepted closure. Any-layer exclusion requires all
searched domains closed; a populated three-column pyramid or an empty/null field is not proof.
Even a complete empty domain needs an accepted zero-denominator/comparability rule for scoring.
Complete-as-reported is a bounded declaration, never a chemical formula or complete human perception.

OP03 can approve a projection proposal without approving canonical promotion. Any future accepted
derived claim needs its own version/locator and original lineage, independent mapping review and
SQL/FK/rights/conflict/materialization/replay/rollback tests. Do not rewrite existing selections,
raw arrays or observations. A sidecar is not hold resolution, closure or matching eligibility.

OP04 must identify intended operations separately: automation, storage, display and any cache,
redistribution, derivative, retention or publication use supported by the relevant contract. Actual
schema fields and permission semantics must be reviewed; this packet adds no right or schema field.
Revoked/expired/unknown uses cannot be compensated by confidence. Metadata licensing does not grant
unrelated bottle/note images. WorkstreamG's source/media plan remains a separate Planning Gate.

OP05 must respect the actual immutable subject and scope model. Shared membership does not convert
a user_private scope into shared data. Synthetic genuine-session tests and an internal DTO do not
authorize actual catalogue application reads or public serialization. No account or role is created.
