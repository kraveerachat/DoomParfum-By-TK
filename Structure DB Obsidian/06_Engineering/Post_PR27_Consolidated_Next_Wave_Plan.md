## State and rights contracts

These state meanings are required for the proposed next integration; they do not retroactively assert completeness for any source:

| State | Evidence meaning | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| Missing | Expected source field is blank or not supplied in the inspected schema/record | Not proof the perfume has no such note |
| Unknown | No usable assertion establishes the property or domain | Never replace with zero, absent, false or complete |
| Unverified | A reported claim exists without the required review/binding | Not canonical truth or eligibility |
| Incomplete | Positive observations exist but the exact searched domain is not closed | No strict absence/exclusion pass from missing membership |
| Conflicting | Incompatible observations or a held relationship need explicit resolution | No newest/majority/default winner |
| Explicitly complete-empty | Reviewed versioned source/capture/edition/domain assertion positively establishes an empty domain | Cannot be synthesized from a blank CSV field or complete synthetic fixture |
| Verified present | Reviewed accessible selected observation and provenance support presence | Does not independently prove source completeness, rights for every use, or matching eligibility |

General-only notes remain general; no pyramid layer is invented. Material categories, note order, repetition and prose do not produce accord strengths. Canonical identity, versioned vocabulary/domain compatibility, closure, selected claims/materialization, scope, active use-specific rights and eligibility are separate gates. Missing any required gate leaves the future real operation unavailable. Structural/Query Fit, Data Confidence and Direct Pairwise Evidence remain separate; fit percentages are not measured smell similarity.

Existing synthetic contracts deliberately identify fixed fixtures. Real catalogue records must not be relabelled SYNTHETIC or assigned fixture fingerprints to bypass those contracts. A future real-data boundary needs its own exact approved contract/task; this wave neither creates nor activates it.

Recommend future **IMP-ENR-01**, an isolated offline candidate-claim preview under `ENR-PINNED-SCENTO-1`, after this Planning PR is Owner-merged and exact A/B/I choices are accepted. The ID is a proposed reservation, not an active implementation task or permission grant.

- Exactly two existing perfume source records, one house and two materials; maximum five records, from pinned revision `086769ecf1add770ea6646bd4d88d39f0e7cddeb` only.
- Source keys and one-based data ordinals excluding the CSV header: `afnan-9-am` 644, `afnan-9-am-dive` 645, house `afnan` 3, material `mint` 257 and `bergamot` 46. Source-file hashes and exact proposed fields/claim types are in the A note.
- Output would be local unselected candidate observations and review diagnostics using the A note's proposed transform version. Preserve original locators, occurrences, unknowns and conflicts. No SQL, import, canonical selection, materialization, normalization, closure assertion, hold release, new source acquisition or matching activation.
- Positive acceptance: exact five locators/hash bindings, typed per-field preview, provenance and attribution, reproducible output under the same transform/input version, replay without duplicate preview entries, dry-run rollback/discard without source mutation.
- Negative acceptance: sixth record or unapproved field/source/revision, altered pin, edition ambiguity, unknown aliases/domains, held/conflicting claims, inferred closure/accords, silent deduplication and unauthorized file/database/network writes all fail or remain explicitly unavailable as specified.

This narrow data milestone does not depend on choosing an application stack, a cloud provider or real matching policy. Conversely, its success cannot authorize media intake or real matching.
