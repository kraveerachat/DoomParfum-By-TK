# Luxury reference design research matrix

Task: [[DPT-PLAN-012_Luxury_Frontend_Editorial]]. Research date: 2026-11-23, Asia/Bangkok. Every adaptation below is PROPOSED, never an observation of DoomParfum.

## Method and evidence limits

Read-only Brave browser inspection through CUA: accessibility trees and rendered viewport captures. All 20 supplied URLs were opened at desktop size (approximately 2033 × 1016 capture pixels; not a calibrated CSS viewport). All 20 were also attempted with a requested 375 × 812 CSS viewport on the active research tab. Early resize captures in other tabs remained desktop-sized and are excluded from mobile evidence. The override was reset and temporary research tabs were closed. No source, photography, logo, browser cookie, account identifier or raw DOM dump was copied into this repository. Screenshot observations are session evidence, not committed reproducible goldens. No site was cloned or scraped in bulk.

Font descriptions are visual classifications, not verified font-family identities. Centering is a visual observation, not proof of exact geometric centering. No reference passed a full accessibility, performance, reduced-motion or keyboard audit. Video/playback controls and changed campaign frames were observed; frame rate, easing, timing and scroll choreography were not measured. PDP = product detail page; PLP = product listing page; NI = not inspected. NI is not evidence of absence. This is a bounded reference study, with shallow home-page coverage and three directly inspected PDPs, not a complete audit of 20 storefronts.

## Synthesis and selection

Most useful composition references: P04 for separation of object and information; P07 for sparse product framing; F03 for calm grid density; F01 for editorial whitespace. These are judgments from the recorded views, not usability evidence. No commercial controls, store locators, shipping gates, purchasing claims or reference brand assets transfer to DoomParfum.

Proposed world: an ivory scent folio, ink reading surfaces, gold as a limited material detail, navy as a subordinate editorial section. A labelled Top / Heart / Base reading sequence is the signature. The requested small left wordmark plus true-centered navigation is a new layout specification; no surveyed screenshot proves its exact geometry. See [[Luxury_Routes_and_Wireframes]], [[Luxury_Design_Tokens_and_Components]] and [[Luxury_Design_Critique_and_Audit]].

Coverage gaps retained for Owner review: most dedicated PLPs/PDPs, reference tablet behavior, actual menu operation, exact font identities, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior, motion timing and performance. AURALEE and Amouage media, Le Labo population and KHAITE mobile remain partial. No facts about those unseen regions are invented. None is required to implement the proposed original layout; additional reference study can be separately scoped if the Owner requires exhaustive site audits.
