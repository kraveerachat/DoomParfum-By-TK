# Matching data readiness, completeness and normalization gates

Owner approval of a new completeness/absence/normalization contract is NOT RECORDED. The existing
adapter deliberately returns reported memberships as known-incomplete, not complete domains.
All44holds and zero real matching flags remain. This proposal does not approve any source claim,
derived observation, materialization, public use or row promotion.

## Proposed accepted-domain contract

Acceptance must be explicit for each method/domain version and selected source claim. A proposed
closure record binds canonical perfume identity/edition, selected observation/capture/field/locator,
source revision/hash, transform/vocabulary version, pyramid layer or flat/general domain, scope,
reviewer/time, exact membership and a separately accepted complete-as-reported assertion. Review
must state whether a deliberately reported empty list is complete. Source silence, null, omitted
fields, an empty database join, list length, three populated columns or review_status=approved is
not that assertion. Complete-as-reported describes the reviewed source declaration, not all chemical
ingredients, actual perceived notes, accords or scientific composition.

| Input state | Proposed strict-rule treatment | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Reviewed reported member present | positive membership only when exact selected/provenance/vocabulary contract passes | complete domain or absence of other notes |
| Explicit accepted complete domain, note absent | negative predicate may pass only inside that exact reviewed domain and active rights | absence in another layer, edition or source |
| Explicit accepted complete empty list | may support scoped absence after accepted empty-domain semantics | automatic zero-valued similarity; both-empty denominator policy remains separate |
| Unknown, incomplete, inaccessible, conflicting or held | strict positive/negative predicates cannot pass; return an explainable unavailable outcome without existence leaks | a false canonical claim or inferred absence |

Exact-layer exclusion needs closure of that layer. Any-layer exclusion needs closure of every
layer/domain that the method searches; unknown general/layer relationships cannot be ignored.
Flattening a complete pyramid is a separate versioned lineage-preserving read transform, not a
claim that general notes disclose top/heart/base. Aliases need reviewed unambiguous canonical
bindings; no fuzzy identity equivalence from punctuation, related brands or note-order repetition.

## Selected-field and operation-specific eligibility

Before a field contributes to a query, explanation, score or count, require exact canonical ID and
edition, genuine authorized scope, approved effective selection, matching field/entity/scope/capture
and transform contract, valid source hashes/revision/locator, approved related vocabulary, and no
relevant unresolved conflict. Canonical materialized values must agree with the selected claim.
Private overlays do not become public facts by referencing a public catalogue entity.

Rights require an applicable current source/record assessment, evidence, checked_at not in the
future, review_after not expired and no revocation. Actual operations need their own allowed use:
local adapter storage/display/automation; cache/redistribution/derivative/publication additionally
require those explicit permissions. Unknown/restricted uses fail closed. Metadata grants never
authorize another source/asset, unsupported JPEG conversion or an unrelated derivative. Retain
publisher/license/attribution/change notice with the authorized internal fact; redact it when the
field is withheld. An internal DTO is not redistribution approval.

matching_eligible=true is necessary under a future accepted policy but never sufficient alone.
It cannot bypass accepted closure, source permissions, session isolation, identity/conflicts or
selected-field requirements. Current1435source-exact-approved profiles therefore remain ranking
