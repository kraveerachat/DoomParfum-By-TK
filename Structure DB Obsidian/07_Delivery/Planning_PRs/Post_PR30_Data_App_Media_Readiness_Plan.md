## Integrated gate requirements

Human review may precede principal implementation. For the recommended clean application-private scope, genuine principal/scope provisioning must precede any separately authorized canonical intake into that scope. Existing immutable operator ownership is not rewritten or bypassed. `dpt_runtime` broad SELECT/guard grants and internal `read_profile` admission are not proof of a least-privilege application catalogue role; the later role/repository contract must address this explicitly without rewriting migrations 001–008.

Authorized reviewed factual display needs identity, selected-field provenance, scope and use rights. It does **not** require full note-domain completeness or matching eligibility; it must project honest unknown/incomplete states. Real matching separately needs accepted real contracts, canonical vocabulary/normalization, closure where required, conflict/hold checks and explicit eligibility. Unknown accord strength stays unavailable. No preview field becomes canonical merely because it is PRESENT.

Media intake and private delivery are separate future tasks. A license URL, source-record relationship, valid PNG or public perfume identity is insufficient to authorize an asset operation. Scope, exact asset/capture/hash, rights-holder evidence, intended action/audience, attribution and current expiry/revocation/lineage checks are independently required. Project suspension of uncertain operations is not a claim that compliant Creative Commons freedoms can be revoked at will.
