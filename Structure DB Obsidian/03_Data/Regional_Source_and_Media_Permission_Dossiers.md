## How to read operation states

States describe the evidence for the exact intended catalogue/media use, not legal opinions about
every possible use. They do not select policies. **L** = historical bounded local snapshot use only;
**C** = documented conditional license/access path, still no executable intake approval;
**E** = published restriction observed for proposed collection/reuse, blocked without compatible
written permission; **U** = UNKNOWN and blocked; **G** = separate Owner/version/use gate;
**R** = existing project assessment restricts the operation. Personal browsing permission, a privacy
notice, no API key, robots visibility, file extension or displayed license label cannot substitute
for field/asset/use/audience evidence. Entries may have narrower published exceptions that do not
establish our proposed automated catalogue/private application use.

## Bottle media and note media are separate decisions

For bottle images, prioritize a permissioned publisher press/export file or actual Owner-created
photograph with exact edition/concentration/packaging evidence. Website image visibility, purchase
of the perfume, retailer authorization and a copyright notice are not per-file permission for our
download/storage/private display/crop/redistribution uses. Manufacturer, retailer and photographer
may control different claims. Owner-created photography still needs creator/other-rights review.

For note illustrations, Commons, a specifically verified Open Access museum asset or commissioned
work can supply a potential illustration route. None proves perfume chemistry or note membership.
Museum botanical/art subject fit must be reviewed; no claim that an appropriate specimen or exact
note illustration is already available. Existing 15 media candidates remain metadata-only/blocked;
no page-revision observation upgrades them to usable images. New media permissions are 0.

The current PNG validator is a technical safety boundary only. A PNG PASS cannot prove authorship,
license or canonical binding. JPEG/WebP support and any crop/resize/format conversion require their
own reviewed security/rights/task scope; Met's documented JPEG references cannot enter the current
PNG lane merely by changing extension. No conversion/decoder/library or image acquisition ran.

An eventual asset dossier must bind source/capture/asset/parent, exact file revision/hash/byte length,
creator and selected license evidence to intended note or perfume edition and access scope. Before
delivery, verify format/MIME/dimensions/technical safety, active permitted operation, required credit,
derivative ceiling and private authorization. Parent revocation or corrected ownership evidence must
propagate to descendants/cache/restored objects under the accepted policy; preserve lawful evidence
and replacement lineage. Public catalogue linkage never publishes a private photo/source/path.

Separate three concepts: API/website access withdrawal, a project rights-review deadline and validity
of a copyright license. Do not invent expiry/revocability for CC0 or imply a publisher can revoke a
valid CC BY grant simply by editing a page. [CC BY4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
preserves licensed freedoms while its conditions are met; [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
does not clear trademarks, privacy or third-party rights. A new ownership complaint or ambiguous
asset binding still blocks our affected use pending review. Keep original license evidence and
record the actual reason; never replace it with an invented legal conclusion.
