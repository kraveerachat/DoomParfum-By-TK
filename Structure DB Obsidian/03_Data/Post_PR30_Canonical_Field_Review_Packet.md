## Identity, semantics and completeness dependencies

The two product identities need independent perfume/house/edition evidence. Neither a source key, similar name, concentration string nor a house link proves edition identity. No `edition_label` source field exists in this 46-slot contract. `dpt.perfumes.edition_label` exists, but this packet cannot populate it. Product variants/SKU/volume are outside scope. Mint and Bergamot are independent material samples; their inclusion does not assert both appear in both perfumes or resolve every note label in either perfume.

Existing seeded `perfume/family` and `perfume/secondary_family` are **string** observations. Existing `perfume/families.primary` and `perfume/families.secondary` require taxonomy **objects**, with `dpt.families(scheme,code,label)` and `dpt.perfume_families`. This proposal preserves raw labels only under the former paths if later authorized. It proposes no string-to-taxonomy mapping, family UUID or scheme. Similarly `note/material_family`, `note/origin`, `note/smell`, `brand/founded` and `brand/type` are seeded source-specific definitions without corresponding materialized catalogue columns. Do not invent those columns or substitute `note/material_origin`, `note/description`, `brand/tags` or perfume accords.

Source `house_id` needs a reviewed canonical `brand_id` UUID crosswalk. Raw `house` corroborates that crosswalk; no `perfume/house` definition exists. Raw `country` is an origin assertion, while `brands.country_code` references uppercase two-letter `dpt.countries.code`; the conversion is unapproved. Raw `id` fields remain source-record keys. Raw `url` fields remain capture locators, never `brands.website_url` merely because that column exists.

Historical selected perfume layer totals are 12 and 11 ordered occurrences; General totals are also 12 and 11. Literal equality with concatenated layers does not authorize derivation, reassignment or completeness. General and layers remain separate original evidence. `validate_model()` forbids simultaneous canonical General and pyramid joins. A future reviewer must choose one materialization representation, without deleting the other raw assertions. Whole-field atomicity does not prove that a publisher supplied a closed domain. No complete Top/Heart/Base/General masks, known-empty set or absence claim is accepted.

Historical alias lists contain 10 and 24 ordered occurrences; no exact stripped-literal overlap was observed. Those facts do not accept equivalence, exhaustive aliases, language, normalized uniqueness or chemical identity. Canonical alias review must assess every occurrence and cross-vocabulary ambiguity under an accepted vocabulary/policy version. Missing concentration/aliases, repeated lists and alias-collision tests require marked synthetic fixtures; those conditions are not observed in the chosen real slots. Raw assertions, canonical note labels, chemical ingredients, perfume accords and similarity evidence remain distinct.
